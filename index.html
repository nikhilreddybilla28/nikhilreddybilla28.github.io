About me

Hi, welcome to my personal homepage! I'm a graduate student in Computer Engineering (M.S.) at Virginia Tech, and a Graduate Research Assistant at the Responsible Data Science Lab. My research focuses on privacy and security in AI—specifically memorization in LLMs, adversarial robustness, and trustworthiness—with the goal of enabling safe and reliable deployment of AI systems.

I have published at WACV and in the Journal of Information Security and Applications. My work spans AI security, computer vision, image forensics, and autonomous navigation (e.g., benchmarks for segmentation in unstructured traffic and adverse weather). I have also collaborated on unsupervised domain adaptation for medical AI with the University of Leicester.

Prior to Virginia Tech, I completed my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from NIT Rourkela and worked as a Software Engineer at NCR Corporation. I serve as a Reviewer for the Journal of Information Security and Applications (JISA). I am actively seeking opportunities to develop AI solutions that prioritize safety, privacy, and real-world impact.

Research Interests

I work on AI security, privacy, and trustworthiness—and their intersection with computer vision and autonomous systems. Current focus areas:

  1. LLM Memorization & Privacy: Investigating memorization risks and privacy implications in large language models to enable safer deployment and reduce leakage of training data.
  2. Adversarial Robustness & Trustworthiness: Improving robustness of AI models against adversarial attacks and building more trustworthy, reliable systems for real-world use.
  3. Computer Vision & Image Forensics: CNN-based techniques for digital integrity verification, image resizing forensics, and double-JPEG detection (e.g., high-pass filter pre-processing with end-to-end networks).
  4. Autonomous Navigation & Segmentation: Benchmarks and models for safe and robust segmentation in unstructured traffic and adverse weather—e.g., IDD-AW (WACV 2024).
  5. Domain Adaptation: Unsupervised domain adaptation for medical AI, including retinal development analysis in collaboration with University of Leicester and IIIT Hyderabad.
