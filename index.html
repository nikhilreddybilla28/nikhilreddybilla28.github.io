Research

My work focuses on AI security, computer vision, and autonomous systems—with an emphasis on privacy, safety, and trustworthiness.

LLM Memorization & Privacy

Investigating memorization risks and privacy implications in large language models to enable safer deployment.

Adversarial Robustness & Trustworthiness

Improving robustness of AI models against adversarial attacks and building more trustworthy systems.

Computer Vision & Image Forensics

CNN-based techniques for digital integrity verification, image resizing forensics, and double-JPEG detection.

Autonomous Navigation & Segmentation

Benchmarks and models for safe segmentation in unstructured traffic and adverse weather (e.g., IDD-AW).

Domain Adaptation

Unsupervised domain adaptation for medical AI (e.g., retinal development analysis in collaboration with University of Leicester).
